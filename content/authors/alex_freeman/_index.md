Alex Freeman is a PhD student in the School of Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering at UNSW Sydney. His work explores how we can use optical measurements to determine the orbits of spacecraft near the Moon. He explores how mathematical tori could be used to represent quasi periodic motion in the Earth-Moon system. These tori act as an atlas to construct admissible regions, a subset of range and range-rate values that are consistent with the optical measurements. By allowing for more realistic motion, his work aims to better describe the uncertainty and improve orbit determination from optical measurements.
