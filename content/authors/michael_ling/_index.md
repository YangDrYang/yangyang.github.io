Michael Ling is a Research Associate at UNSW Sydney working on the NSW Space Research Network's Pattern of Life Identification for Geosynchronous Satellites project. His research uses machine-learning methods to characterise GEO satellite behaviour and support satellite manoeuvre detection and space domain awareness.

His previous research applied multiple sets of orbital proper elements and machine learning to classify low-Earth-orbit debris families.
