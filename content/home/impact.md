### Results on the record

- **Debris family classification published in IEEE TAES.** In a synthetic, atmospheric-drag-free LEO
  benchmark, MEE + QTNp achieved ROC-AUC 0.656, while QTNp achieved the highest positive-class F1
  (0.1245, versus 0.1023 for standard QTN). The threshold-tuned F1 is not an operational performance
  estimate.
  [Journal paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.2026.3737763)
- **Autonomous lunar orbit determination to approximately 11 m in 3-D position and 1.58 mm/s in
  velocity** using inter-satellite links alone, with no direct ground support to the navigation
  satellite (CR3BP case); approximately 1 km and 10 mm/s under a high-fidelity dynamical model after a
  two-day ground-tracking handover.
  [MPhil thesis, UNSW Sydney](https://unsworks.unsw.edu.au/entities/publication/ef9a27ce-1bee-4219-972b-b3f8d3db42b6)
- **Lunar navigation constellation optimised for south-pole coverage**, integrating a semi-analytical
  station-keeping model with Earth-GPS for time synchronisation and ephemeris generation.
  [*Acta Astronautica* **239**, 278–293 (2026)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0094576525007465)
- **UNSW Observatory automated for remote satellite tracking**, converting a heritage instrument into a
  taskable sensor for space object tracking and characterisation.
  [Project page](/project/optical-tracking/) ·
  [thesis slides](/slides/Observatory_Automation_and_Remote_Control.pdf)
