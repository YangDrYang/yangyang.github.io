I attended the 77th International Astronautical Congress (IAC) in Antalya, Türkiye, from 5–9 October 2026. It was wonderful to reconnect with friends and colleagues from around the world and make new connections across the global space community.

I’m also delighted that our new PhD student, Sibtain Ali Thepdawala, was selected as one of the 2026 IAF Emerging Space Leaders. After graduating from the Technical University of Munich (TUM), Sibtain has now joined us to pursue his PhD. Congratulations, Sibtain!
