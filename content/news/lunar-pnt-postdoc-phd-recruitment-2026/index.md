We are recruiting for an ARC Discovery Project developing next-generation technologies for lunar positioning, navigation and timing (Lunar PNT) at UNSW Sydney.

## Opportunities

- **3-year Postdoctoral Fellow in Lunar Positioning and Timing**
- **Fully funded 3-year PhD scholarship**, including a stipend at the current UNSW PhD scholarship rate (A$39,206 per annum tax-free, 2026 rate) and tuition-fee support for eligible candidates.

The project is supervised by [Professor Andrew Dempster](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrew-dempster-798a299/) and Dr Yang Yang. It offers the opportunity to work on cutting-edge research in:

- Positioning, navigation and timing (PNT/GNSS)
- Estimation, filtering, and sensor fusion
- Spacecraft navigation and autonomous systems
- Cislunar dynamics (desirable, but not essential)

We welcome applicants from space engineering, electrical engineering, robotics, applied mathematics, and related disciplines with strong analytical and research skills.

**Location:** UNSW Sydney, Australia  
**Postdoctoral applications close:** 1 September 2026  
**PhD applications:** Open until the position is filled

[Apply for the Postdoctoral Fellow position.](https://lnkd.in/gryAYsFp)

For either opportunity, please contact Professor Andrew Dempster or Dr Yang Yang. Please share this opportunity with outstanding researchers and students interested in shaping the future of lunar navigation.
