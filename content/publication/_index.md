## Selected work

- **Supervised classification of LEO debris families using multi-set proper elements.** Michael Ling and
  Yang Yang. Preprint, [arXiv:2512.08495](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08495).
  Improves debris family classification to ROC-AUC 0.858 against a 0.789 baseline by combining
  modified equinoctial, Poincaré and quaternion proper-element sets.

- **[Lunar navigation system optimization for targeted coverage with semi-analytical station keeping model and Earth-GPS integration](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0094576525007465).**
  Rameez A. Malik and Yang Yang. *Acta Astronautica* **239**, 278–293 (February 2026).
  doi:[10.1016/j.actaastro.2025.10.065](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actaastro.2025.10.065).
  Multi-objective optimisation of a lunar navigation satellite system for the lunar south pole.
  → [Lunar and cislunar navigation](/project/space-navigation/)

- **Square-root higher-order unscented estimators.** *IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic
  Systems.* Estimation methods underpinning the orbit determination work across these projects.
